Research Assistant Program

The Global Macro Lab at Johns Hopkins, led by Prof. Domenico Giannone, offers undergraduate and master’s students the opportunity to work as research assistants on its projects in macroeconomics and finance. The program is for students at the intersection of economics, applied mathematics and statistics who want to work on forecasting with big data, machine learning and large language models. Research assistants join a small team led by one of the lab’s postdoctoral researchers and contribute a few hours a week to active research.
