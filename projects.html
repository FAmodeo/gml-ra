P1 — Global Macro News Index

Project description. Markets, policymakers and firms follow a steady flow of macroeconomic news: new data on growth, jobs and inflation, and how they compare with what forecasters expected. The lab is building a Global Macro News Index, a measure that summarizes this news across countries and over time, and studies how financial markets respond to it. The project is at an early stage, and its design will evolve as we learn.

What to expect. Students start by reading the relevant research and learning the data and the tools. Each then takes on a well-defined piece of the work, such as collecting and cleaning data on releases, forecasts and asset prices, running first analyses in Python, or reviewing the literature. Tasks adapt as the project develops. Basic Python and an introductory course in statistics or econometrics, or the willingness to learn Python in the first weeks. Interest in macroeconomics or finance helps.
