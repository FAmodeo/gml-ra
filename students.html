research assistant program

For students

You join a research project in macroeconomics or finance, in a small team led by a postdoctoral researcher of the Global Macro Lab. Applications are open year-round.

What you do

  • A few hours of work a week, around five as a common guide, agreed with your lead.
  • Regular team meetings, usually for a semester or longer.
  • Concrete tasks: collecting and cleaning data, building datasets and code, checking series against official sources, reading literature, making charts and documentation.

What you get

  • Hands-on work on real research, and the skills that come with it: data collection, data cleaning, programming.
  • Close mentorship from a postdoctoral researcher.
  • An inside view of research careers, including graduate school and pre-doctoral positions.
  • A mentor who knows your work and can write a reference letter.

Who can join

Johns Hopkins undergraduate and master’s students from any department, especially those at the intersection of economics, applied mathematics and statistics who want to work on forecasting with big data, machine learning and large language models. Each project lists the skills it needs.

How to join

  1. Read the projects and pick one that interests you.

  2. Fill in the interest form (JHU login): your name, school and year; your major; relevant courses and skills (which programming languages, at what level); which project and why; how many hours a week you can give.

    Interest formJHU login

  3. A short conversation with the project lead to discuss the fit.

  4. Join the team: regular meetings and a few hours of work a week.

Not sure which project fits you? Say so in the form and we will suggest one.

Use of AI

AI tools are welcome for help with code and for checking your writing. You stay the author of your work: you should understand every line of code and every sentence you hand in, and be able to explain it to your lead. Tell your lead which tools you used and for what. Analysis or reports written by AI, which you cannot explain yourself, are not accepted.

Academic credit

You can take part as a volunteer or for academic credit, through an Independent Study supervised by Prof. Giannone. Indicate your interest in the form.
